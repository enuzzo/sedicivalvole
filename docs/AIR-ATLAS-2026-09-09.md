## Runtime

- Lazy Visual 10, excluded from Lucky. No hidden renderer is mounted during preload.
- `radar-data.php` queries only `https://api.adsb.lol`: nearby traffic at a two-decimal user location and 27 nautical miles; route at the selected aircraft's callsign and three-decimal aircraft position. Strict query allowlist, canonical origin/custom-header checks, TLS validation, no redirects, identifying project User-Agent and 128 KiB response ceiling.
- Private temporary shared cache: at most 32 entries, eight seconds for traffic and five minutes for routes, one upstream request at a time. Failure cooldown honors the supplied Retry-After within a one-day server bound. No persistent location history or diagnostic coordinate fields are added. Standard server access logs may contain the rounded query; the cache stores hashed query keys and bounded response data.
- One client poll every eight seconds; cancellation on unmount, hidden/offline pause, bounded retry up to five minutes and foreground/online recovery without overlap. Only the selected aircraft triggers photo/route detail. Metadata cache holds at most 64 entries for ten minutes in page memory.
- At most 32 nearby measured aircraft per snapshot; stale indications after 30 seconds, expiration after 120 seconds. Position interpolation ends at the latest received observation, never extrapolating beyond it. Unknown type/direction uses a neutral dot. Aircraft targets remain 48 px.
- Zoom in/out and reset to location/default zoom. North-up or GPS driving-up, with a home marker. Driving direction requires speed above 3 km/h and a GPS observation younger than 15 seconds. Missing direction falls back visibly to north; no parked vehicle compass orientation is inferred. Camera center/bearing update together and aircraft shapes rotate with the map.
- Bottom bar expands to credited photo, full departure/arrival airport names, aircraft model, distance, ground track, vertical rate and ICAO address. Route plausibility is labelled, not represented as a confirmed flight plan. Unknown values remain unknown.

## First location, observed trails and subtle lens — 2026-09-10

The misleading Location required message came from a position-only gate: the shared trusted map history rejects accuracy worse than 250 m, while Air Atlas did not receive GPS or permission state. Air Atlas now has a separate initial display fallback for valid coordinates at any reported accuracy, independent of speed. A trusted fix takes over without restarting the map. Coarse location and associated marker distance are labelled approximate; trusted motion, journey, altitude and diagnostic filters remain unchanged. Retained position becomes explicitly last known after 30 seconds without update.

Location permission is read through the optional browser Permissions API and its change event. Granted access awaiting a fix, denied access, unsupported geolocation and unknown permission are distinct. Unsupported Permissions queries are caught; the geolocation callback is still authoritative for obtaining a display fix. This uses the same application callback on desktop and Tesla; browser tests do not prove physical Tesla reception. Permission grants access, not proof of a GPS satellite lock: see [MDN Geolocation API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Geolocation_API).

Aircraft trails use received observations only: at most 5 km, five minutes and 256 samples per aircraft, ending at the same delayed sample as its icon. A gap over 15 seconds, implausible jump or date-line crossing breaks the line; no route before opening the view is invented. A single GeoJSON layer updates at most four times per second with 1.5 px dashes at 32% opacity, using the active accent.

Owner-selected direction A adds a 5.5% radial lens to the existing map canvas, including cartography and trails. A small WebGL compositor copies only during MapLibre render; the matching forward mapping offsets the real aircraft/home DOM markers and their hit regions (under 11 px at 773 x 601). Controls and text panels remain flat. The inset uses the same lens; the reconstructed nose camera remains its real terrain view. No upstream runtime or new third-party dependency is changed. Context failure/loss restores the ordinary map. This is an artistic lens, not geographic Earth curvature; native pan/zoom remains MapLibre navigation. It adds a GPU compositing pass, so sustained vehicle frame rate requires physical acceptance.
