## Runtime

- Lazy Visual 10, excluded from Lucky. No hidden renderer is mounted during preload.
- `radar-data.php` queries only `https://api.adsb.lol`: nearby traffic at a two-decimal user location and 27 nautical miles; route at the selected aircraft's callsign and three-decimal aircraft position. Strict query allowlist, canonical origin/custom-header checks, TLS validation, no redirects, identifying project User-Agent and 128 KiB response ceiling.
- Private temporary shared cache: at most 32 entries, eight seconds for traffic and five minutes for routes, one upstream request at a time. Failure cooldown honors the supplied Retry-After within a one-day server bound. No persistent location history or diagnostic coordinate fields are added. Standard server access logs may contain the rounded query; the cache stores hashed query keys and bounded response data.
- One client poll every eight seconds; cancellation on unmount, hidden/offline pause, bounded retry up to five minutes and foreground/online recovery without overlap. Only the selected aircraft triggers photo/route detail. Metadata cache holds at most 64 entries for ten minutes in page memory.
- At most 32 nearby measured aircraft per snapshot; stale indications after 30 seconds, expiration after 120 seconds. Position interpolation ends at the latest received observation, never extrapolating beyond it. Unknown type/direction uses a neutral dot. Aircraft targets remain 48 px.
- Zoom in/out and reset to location/default zoom. North-up or GPS driving-up, with a home marker. Driving direction requires speed above 3 km/h and a GPS observation younger than 15 seconds. Missing direction falls back visibly to north; no parked vehicle compass orientation is inferred. Camera center/bearing update together and aircraft shapes rotate with the map.
- Bottom bar expands to credited photo, full departure/arrival airport names, aircraft model, distance, ground track, vertical rate and ICAO address. Route plausibility is labelled, not represented as a confirmed flight plan. Unknown values remain unknown.
