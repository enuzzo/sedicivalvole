## Visual direction corrections

The current luminous image treatment is rejected as a product direction. The earlier selection of direction 1 preserves only its useful structural ideas: a central convergence point, depth, speed response, and a low control plane. It does not approve the current lens-flare-heavy rendering, color treatment, or static texture.

Confirmed requirements:

- minimal, precise, powerful, and Swiss-influenced rather than decorative or cinematic-wallpaper-like;
- no lens flares, starburst wallpaper, noisy light waves, illustrated scenery, or nostalgic Flash aesthetics;
- a genuinely generative field rather than a static image with displacement;
- calm and comparatively flat at rest;
- progressively stretched, accelerated, and drawn toward a center funnel/tunnel as energy rises;
- an intelligible release back toward a flatter field during deceleration;
- motion driven by the normalized speed/energy stream with controlled smoothing, bounds, and reduced-motion behavior;
- visual energy that supports the audio without obscuring controls or competing with driving information;
- compact, highly legible typography and Tesla-harmonious interaction behavior at the verified `773 x 601` split viewport;
- no visual implementation is approved until exactly three revised minimal directions are presented and one is selected.

The Infinite Lights reference is a study source for depth construction, instancing, road-relative motion, curvature, FOV response, and speed interpolation. It is not a target skin and must not be copied wholesale.

Later direction confirms a multi-environment Flux model: users should be able
to select a coordinated generative effect, musical genre, and palette, with
curated geometry variation where the renderer supports it. Interstate 4 and
Interstate 7 are requested mechanics studies for two contrasting environment
families. Their apparent demo speed represents full energy; the product version
must add continuous speed control, reversible deceleration, palette selection,
and bounded geometry parameters. Three revised directions were generated from
those references on 2026-08-26; selection remains pending before implementation.

## Flux audio correction

The current synthesized spike is technically audible but musically rejected. A continuous oscillator or a collection of isolated electronic pings is not an acceptable interpretation of speed-reactive music.

Confirmed intent:

- “play the road” as an adaptive sampler, arrangement, and performance system;
- build energy with kick, low end, percussion, rhythmic detail, timbral movement, harmonic tension, spatial depth, fills, and controlled effects;
- support strong electronic identities closer in ambition to a changing racing-game score than to a metronome or notification system;
- allow both beat-driven environments and beatless digital/spacecraft-like environments;
- make acceleration perceptible immediately while avoiding a crude linear BPM race;
- use a tempo knee and ceiling, then create most high-speed intensity through arrangement, dynamics, density, register, timbre, and space;
- use quantized entries, crossfades, dwell, and hysteresis so GPS variation does not cause nervous musical changes;
- preserve a restrained continuous energy bed beneath the arrangement, but never let it become an exposed whine or masking noise;
- make deceleration musically meaningful through release, subtraction, filters, tails, and occasional bounded transition accents.

A separate research task must study authoritative material about BMW IconicSounds Electric and Hans Zimmer's role, plus relevant interactive-music techniques. Research informs the system but does not justify imitating protected recordings or compositions.

## Control-model correction

The prototype controls `Atmos`, `Harmonics`, and `Pulse` do not currently communicate a useful user model and are not accepted as final product controls.

Recommended product model:

- expose curated audiovisual environments instead of asking the user to mix low-level implementation layers while driving;
- keep Stop/Mute immediate and unambiguous;
- make the principal performance control an **energy threshold**: the vehicle speed at which the selected environment reaches full intended energy, for example a lower urban range or a higher motorway range;
- retain hue or color-character control where it produces a clear visual choice;
- move advanced layer mixing, if retained, to a parked configuration flow rather than the primary driving surface;
- do not expose a permanent `Brake` button without a clear user purpose. The keyboard Space event remains valid for deterministic testing, and real deceleration may produce a bounded musical/visual event.

## Tesla and delivery requirements

- Target modern Tesla displays such as the Highland/Juniper generation with responsive behavior rather than one assumed universal viewport.
- Treat the measured split view as the primary driving layout and the expanded browser as a secondary configuration/diagnostic layout.
- Keep diagnostics reachable in compact mode; do not require fullscreen to discover or open them.
- Continue to commit and push verified checkpoints and deploy user-approved product changes to the canonical live root.
- Update project instructions and decision records when real vehicle evidence changes an assumption.
